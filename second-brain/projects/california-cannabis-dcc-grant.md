## The methodological opportunity (THC quantification)

The grant could fund establishing **validated quantitative THC testing** on DCLA's existing
Bruker ALPHA II FTIR — scan every sample in-house, send a subset to an accredited HPLC lab for
reference, train a PLS regression model, and progressively reduce reliance on outsourced testing.
This would be **first in the US** to do chemometric FTIR quantification in harm reduction, and
**first anywhere** for cannabis THC.

Full analysis (build plan, precedent, chemistry, CA regulatory context, costs):
