# Drug Checking Los Angeles (DCLA)

The umbrella organization beneath much of the research work: the [cannabis DCC grant](california-cannabis-dcc-grant.md),
the [fentanyl dabbing paper](fentanyl-dabbing-paper.md), the [MME](../knowledge/mme-estimation.md)
and [purity](../knowledge/fentanyl-purity-market-units.md) research, and the drug-checking
methodology work. Nonprofit; structurally positioned to produce trustworthy (non-inflated) numbers.

Ingested from the **DCLA Work Products** board artifact.

## Instrument

Bruker **ALPHA II** with Platinum ATR (single-reflection diamond crystal). Currently used for
qualitative street-drug ID. The [THC/FTIR briefing](../knowledge/thc-ftir-quantification.md)
explores turning it into a quantitative potency tool.
