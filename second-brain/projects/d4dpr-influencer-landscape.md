## The synthesis (as it stands)

Drive drafts: *"Influencer Landscape Narrative — DRAFT v2 social-enriched"* (2026-07-18) and the
earlier DRAFT (2026-07-17). Also a *Misinfo Toolkit* draft. Source base: **64 mapped creators /
orgs / opposition actors** + ranked reach analysis; **two creator convenings** (May 2026, 12
creators, ~3.4M combined followers); **four state stakeholder calls** (Maine, New Jersey, New York,
Kentucky, spring 2026); provider outreach (D4DPR); supplementary July 2026 web-search verification.

**Four orienting findings:**
1. The most credible pro-harm-reduction voices are **individuals, not institutions** — audiences
   trust a nurse with lived experience or a mom describing her son's overdose far more than org
   accounts.
2. The **institutional wing** of harm reduction has modest online reach.
3. The **opposition is smaller but better networked and better resourced.**
4. Method limitation: no TikTok/Meta platform API access, so findings lean on convenings, mapping,
   stakeholder calls, and publicly surfaced content — not systematic platform-level analysis. State
   advocates' local knowledge was indispensable.
