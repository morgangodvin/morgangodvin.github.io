# MME Estimation (LA Fentanyl)

The clinical morphine-milligram-equivalent (MME) estimation research — companion to the LA
[fentanyl purity work](fentanyl-purity-market-units.md). Preprint: **PMC13015637**.
Site has `mme-plain-language-summary.pdf`.

## Core finding

47 LA drug-checking participants averaged **8,887 MME/day** (range 156–41,761 MME), using
**1.07 g/day** raw product. Derived by combining self-reported use with LC-MS purity data across
**10,000 bootstrap iterations**.

## Why it's methodologically hard (the caveats that shape the work)

- **Consumer, not seizure, purity** is the right input (DEA seizure figures overstate by 4–5×).
- **Formulation** is a major dose predictor: LA powder median 6.9%, black tar 1.4%, pills 0.7%.
- **Sold-as** matters: sold-as-fentanyl 7.0% vs sold-as-heroin-but-fentanyl-positive 1.4% (5×).
- **Purity drifts over time** — any tool must show the data date.
- **Within-batch variability** is ~25% RSD ("hot spots").
- **Postmortem levels can't be back-calculated** to antemortem dose (3–5.5× redistribution in 24h).

## Clinical translation

The point of the MME numbers is bedside: clinicians estimating tolerance for a person using illicit
fentanyl need (a) the form used, (b) consumer-level purity for that market, (c) honest uncertainty
bands. The plain-language summary PDF is the public-facing artifact.
